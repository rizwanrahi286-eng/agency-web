Weblitex Privacy & Cookie Policy

At Weblitex Agency, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our web development platforms and customer touchpoints.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our portfolio or contact us via our inquiry forms, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and project details exclusively for communication, quotation, and project scoping purposes.

2. How We Use Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored on your browser that help us:

  • Remember your display preferences and cookie consent status.
  • Ensure lightning-fast page loading and responsive animations.
  • Analyze anonymous engagement metrics to continuously enhance our project presentations.

3. Third-Party Services & Security

We implement enterprise-grade encryption and SSL standards. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
